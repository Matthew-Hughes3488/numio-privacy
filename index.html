Privacy policy

Draft updated 20 September 2026

Pre-launch draft for review. This page is not yet the final policy for a public release. Retention enforcement, billing retention, provider storage and international transfers, and arrangements for younger users still need confirmation. Approved future commitments are identified below.

The short version

Numio helps you understand maths. To do that, it sends the questions and photos you submit to an AI service, saves your work, and checks your subscription when needed. We also collect information about how the app is used.

Please photograph just the question. Avoid including your name, school, face, address or other private information. A photo can contain more than the maths on the page.

Numio is operated by Matthew Hughes, who is responsible for its handling of your personal information. You can contact him at 12hughesm@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: a guest or account identifier, your optional preferred name, sign-in provider identifiers, and information supplied through Apple or Google sign-in. This may include an email address and name, depending on the provider and permissions.
  • Your learning content: typed questions, submitted photos, extracted question text, solutions, follow-up questions, tutor replies, saved history and learning preferences.
  • Subscription information: products, purchase and renewal events, subscription status, and identifiers used to connect a subscription to your account. Apple processes App Store payments; Numio does not collect your payment-card details.
  • Usage and technical information: actions in the app, request status, usage totals, errors, app and device information, and account-linked analytics identifiers. Network providers also process connection information needed to handle requests.
  • Support information: anything you choose to include when you contact us.

Guest use also creates an identifier. Guest information and account-linked analytics are not necessarily anonymous.

Why the information is used

We use this information to recognise a photographed question, generate explanations, answer follow-up questions, save your work, manage sign-in and subscriptions, provide support, measure how features are used, diagnose failures and prevent misuse.

Proposed legal bases, subject to pre-launch review: providing the service you request where necessary to perform a contract; legitimate interests in security, reliability and service improvement where those interests do not override your rights; legal obligations for records we are required to retain; and consent where required. Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. The appropriate arrangements for children and analytics must be confirmed before launch.

Services that receive information

Supabase — accounts and saved work
Hosts authentication, the backend and stored learning information, including questions, solutions and conversations. Supabase privacy policy.
OpenAI — reading photos and explaining maths
Receives submitted photos for extraction, and question text and relevant solution or conversation context for explanations. It receives the image itself, including any personal information visible in it. Numio's backend does not keep the submitted photos. The current integration disables response storage for photo extraction but enables it for generation. OpenAI documents default abuse-monitoring retention of up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal or safety needs, and a default 30-day period for stored responses. Disabling response storage does not disable all provider retention. Account-specific controls and deletion arrangements remain to be confirmed. See OpenAI's API data controls and privacy policy.
RevenueCat — subscription management
Receives an app user identifier and subscription and purchase information to manage access. An analytics identifier connects relevant subscription events with PostHog. The integration does not intentionally attach your homework, photos, name or email to RevenueCat. RevenueCat privacy policy.
PostHog — app analytics
Receives app events, account-linked identifiers and technical information to help us understand usage and failures. The app's event integration does not intentionally send homework content, photos, names or email addresses. Session replay and automatic screen capture events are disabled in the current configuration. PostHog privacy policy.
Apple and Google — sign-in; Apple — purchases
Process information when you use their sign-in services. Apple also processes App Store transactions. Their own privacy notices explain their handling of information: Apple and Google.

Information may also be disclosed where necessary to comply with applicable law or protect users and the service. Provider privacy notices supplement this page; they do not replace Numio's responsibility for its own use of your information.

Storage and account deletion

Saved questions and conversations are retained to make your learning history available. You can request account deletion in the app or contact us for help. The account-deletion workflow removes the profile and saved learning content from the active database and requests removal of the sign-in identity and identified analytics data. Provider cleanup may complete separately if a request needs retrying.

Submitted photos are sent for processing rather than kept as a photo library on Numio's server. Temporary copies may exist on your device and with the processing provider. Deleting a Numio account is not a promise of immediate removal from all provider systems or backups.

Approved retention policy: minimal usage records retained for abuse prevention will be deleted 90 days after account deletion. These records contain account identifiers and usage totals, rather than question or conversation content. Automated enforcement of that period still needs to be implemented or verified before launch.

Approved accounting retention policy: records required for UK sole-trader tax reporting will be retained for five years after the 31 January filing deadline for the relevant tax year, or longer where a specific legal requirement applies. This period does not automatically apply to every account-linked subscription event. The retention schedule for other operational billing records, backups, technical logs, guest data and provider-held records remains to be finalised before this policy becomes effective.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel it through your Apple account's subscription settings.

Where information is processed

The services used by Numio may process information outside the UK. The actual hosting regions, transfer destinations and applicable safeguards must be confirmed before release. You can contact us for information about the arrangements once confirmed.

Younger users and families

Numio is intended to help learners, including children. A parent, guardian or young person can contact us with privacy questions or requests. Children have privacy rights too.

This draft does not claim that age checks or parental-consent arrangements are already in place. Age-appropriate privacy information, the lawful basis for children's information, any required parental consent, and suitable safeguards need to be settled before making the app available to children.

OpenAI's guidance says personal data of children under 13, or below the applicable digital-consent age, should not be processed without Zero Data Retention enabled. Numio's project has not yet been verified to meet that requirement. This must be resolved before offering the current AI flow to those users. See OpenAI's guidance for services used by minors.

Your choices and rights

You can choose typed entry instead of submitting a photo, manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings, and request account deletion. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a portable copy of your personal information, or object to processing. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

Email 12hughesm@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to verify that the information belongs to you. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or the relevant regulator where you live.
